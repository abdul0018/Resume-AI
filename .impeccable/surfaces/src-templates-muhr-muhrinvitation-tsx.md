# Oltin muhr — wax-seal invitation template

Scope: one invitation template (first of seven) inside the examples-only showcase. Visitor mode: Experience — the invitation itself leads from the first viewport; interface recedes.

Audience: Uzbek couples watching the owner's Instagram recordings on a phone; wedding guests opening the link once. Action: tap the seal, watch it open, scroll the details, answer the RSVP. Content: sample couple, Uzbek wedding custom (Bismillah line, hosting families, nikoh to‘yi, weekday + time, program, venue, RSVP). Constraints: single self-contained page, no map iframes, drawn imagery only (no AI paintings, no people), smooth in Instagram's in-app browser.

Pinned by the owner: the reference video's object and choreography (embossed ivory envelope, wax seal with gold monogram, seal glow + gold dust, flaps open on warm light, names fade in, gold arch scene with domes and lanterns). Uzbek translation: Samarkand domes, carved ganch arch.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The phone screen is the envelope itself, edge to edge, sealed with wax; breaking the seal lets Samarkand's evening light out. Refuses the category default of a centred card floating on a background with a "tap to open" button.

OWN-WORLD: Ivory paper with blind-embossed florals (white-on-white relief, no ink); cream wax seal with gold-leaf monogram; gold leaf from deep antique to pale champagne; warm amber light; carved ganch (alabaster) arch with gilded edge; sepia ink, never black. Type: Corinthia script for names, Bodoni Moda (optical sizes) for everything set. Ornament is relief and light, not colour.

STORY: The guest sees a sealed letter addressed to them, breaks the seal, is welcomed into a lit Samarkand evening with the couple's names, then reads when, where and what happens, and answers.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Closed: full-bleed envelope, top V-flap meeting the seal at 48% height, bottom flap rising to it, embossed rose medallion above the seal and bouquet below, vine borders on the side flaps; seal 34% of width at centre; one italic line under it, "Muhrni bosing". Open: carved arch filling the viewport, domes and minarets in gold haze inside it, two lanterns hanging, names set large in the arch's sky, music control bottom-right.

FORM: Owner-pinned reference video governs the direction (a pinned brief beats the roll); roll recorded for the contract, candidate 1 of 1. seed:ed9697efad0cfcc5
Signature interaction: seal press → glow bloom + canvas gold dust → top flap lifts, side and bottom flaps part on amber light → light bloom → arch scene settles. Motion grammar: slow exponential ease-out, light before movement, one orchestrated moment.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
