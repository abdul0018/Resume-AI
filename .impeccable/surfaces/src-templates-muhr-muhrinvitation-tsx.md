# Oltin muhr — wax-seal invitation template

Scope: one invitation template (first of seven) inside the examples-only showcase. Visitor mode: Experience — the invitation itself leads from the first viewport; interface recedes.

Audience: Uzbek couples watching the owner's Instagram recordings on a phone; wedding guests opening the link once. Action: tap the seal, watch it open, scroll the details, answer the RSVP. Content: sample couple, Uzbek wedding custom (Bismillah line, hosting families, nikoh to‘yi, weekday + time, program, venue, RSVP). Constraints: single self-contained page, no map iframes, smooth in Instagram's in-app browser. Revision 2 (owner request: "exactly the same as the video"): imagery is owner-generated AI art supplied by the owner (hero photograph with the couple, embossed paper, wax seal, rose spray, arch card, rings drawing); remaining timeline drawings are coded gold line art until the owner supplies them.

Pinned by the owner: the reference video's object and choreography (embossed ivory envelope, wax seal with gold monogram, seal glow + gold dust, flaps open on warm light, names fade in, gold arch scene with domes and lanterns). Uzbek translation: Samarkand domes, carved ganch arch.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The phone screen is the envelope itself, edge to edge, sealed with wax; breaking the seal lets Samarkand's evening light out. Refuses the category default of a centred card floating on a background with a "tap to open" button.

OWN-WORLD: Ivory blind-embossed paper (owner photograph) closed with a cream wax seal (owner photograph) whose empty centre carries the couple's initials in pressed gold; warm sunset photograph of the couple and a Tashkent mosque; cream ground #F5ECDF; gold #9A6E2E–#B8924A for script and line art; sepia ink. Type: Corinthia script for names and section titles, Bodoni Moda for set text, Amiri for the Bismillah. Ornament is gold line art, rose sprays (multiply on cream) and the gilded arch card with jali pillar.

STORY: The guest breaks the seal, is welcomed in the sunset sky above the couple, scratches gold foil to reveal the date, reads the arch card naming both families, a short warm message, the program, the venue, then answers.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Closed: full-bleed embossed paper with flap folds meeting the seal at 48% height; seal 36% of width; "Muhrni bosing" below. Open: the couple photograph full-bleed, welcome lines and names set in the sky (upper third, right of the flowering branch), rose sprays at the bottom corners fading into cream, "Pastga suring" and the gold music coin at the foot.

FORM: Owner-pinned reference video governs the direction (a pinned brief beats the roll); roll recorded for the contract, candidate 1 of 1. seed:ed9697efad0cfcc5
Signature interaction: seal press → glow + canvas gold dust → flaps open on amber light → names in light → bloom → photograph settles; second interaction: scratching the gold foil date cards. Motion grammar: slow exponential ease-out, light before movement, one orchestrated opening.
