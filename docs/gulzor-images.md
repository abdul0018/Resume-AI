# Gulzor template: images and opening film

The second template ("Gulzor", the flower garden) uses a cream, sage and blush palette, photographs of white
garden roses, script names and thin line icons. These are the pictures it needs.

Generate them in this order:

1. the hero;
2. the envelope;
3. the film, which uses both;
4. the rest.

Use the same image model for every picture. Where the tool takes a reference image, give it the hero, so the
colours and the light match. Send the files as they come out; any size works. They get cropped,
colour-matched and compressed when they are added.

**Style line.** End every image prompt with this:

> Fine-art garden wedding photography, soft hazy natural daylight, muted pastel palette of ivory, cream, sage
> green and a touch of blush pink, delicate film grain, airy and romantic, photorealistic. No people, no text,
> no letters, no watermark, no logo.

---

## 1. Hero (9:16, 1080×1920)

This is the first screen after the opening, and the film's last frame.

> Vertical photograph of an outdoor garden wedding ceremony, looking straight down the aisle. A lush arch of
> climbing white garden roses, white hydrangeas and trailing greenery frames the top and the left side of the
> picture. Light wooden chairs decorated with small bunches of white roses line a pale stone path that leads
> away into the distance. Behind: tall dark-green cypress trees and soft misty blue hills under a pale sky.
> Big clusters of white roses fill the bottom-left corner. The middle of the picture, from a quarter to three
> quarters of its height, is calm, slightly darker green foliage and trees without bright spots, so white text
> placed there reads clearly.

## 2. Envelope (9:16, 1080×1920)

This is the closed invitation, the film's first frame. The couple's names are set on it by the website.

> Top-down flat-lay photograph, camera exactly overhead. A closed cream-white gatefold wedding invitation card
> (two doors meeting in a straight vertical seam down the centre) lies on soft sage-green linen and fills about
> 85% of the frame's width and 80% of its height. Across the middle of the card runs a slim horizontal line of
> fresh flowers from edge to edge: small white garden roses, white spray roses, jasmine, tiny white blossoms,
> and silvery eucalyptus and olive leaves. A thin sage-green silk ribbon holds them, tied in a small bow on the
> centre seam. The card's upper third and lower third are plain, blank cream paper with a fine deckled edge,
> with nothing printed on them. Soft even daylight from the upper left, with gentle natural shadows.

## 3. Opening film (image to video, 9:16)

- **Model:** one that takes a start frame and an end frame (Kling, Veo, Runway, Higgsfield).
- **Start frame:** the envelope (2).
- **End frame:** the hero (1).
- **Length:** 10 seconds, or the longest the model allows, at 1080p.

Prompt:

> The camera stays perfectly still above the invitation. The small sage silk bow at the centre loosens and
> unties by itself. The line of fresh flowers parts at the seam, and its two halves slide slowly off the card to
> the left and to the right, a few white petals drifting free. Then the left door of the invitation lifts at its
> edge and swings open, followed by the right door, revealing inside a photograph of a garden wedding arch of
> white roses. The camera glides down into the photograph until it fills the whole frame, and the garden comes
> alive: roses and leaves sway in a light breeze and soft sunlight shimmers, until it settles into the final
> frame. Realistic paper, silk, flower and petal physics; slow, smooth and elegant.

Negative prompt:

> hands, fingers, people, faces, text, letters, names, logo, watermark, buttons, camera shake, flicker,
> morphing, warping, cartoon

Use the clean hero picture as the end frame, not a screenshot of the website, so no names or music button get
baked into the film. If the model writes text at the end anyway, send the film as it is: it gets cut before
the text appears.

> the whole width and fill only the top third of the frame. Everything below is a plain, pure white

## 9. Map (1:1)

This is the illustrated map; the website pins the venue on it.

> Hand-painted watercolour illustration of a map, seen from directly above: a green garden estate with winding
> pale beige-pink paths, round watercolour trees in sage and olive greens, small flower beds, a small blue pond
> in the lower part, and a white manor house with a grey roof exactly in the centre. Soft and airy, on cream
> watercolour paper. No text, no labels, no pins, no compass, no border.
