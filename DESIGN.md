# Design System: Oltin muhr

Scope: this file records the first of seven invitation worlds in the examples-only showcase, as shipped in revision 4 (the opening re-made after the owner's ribbon reference video, `.impeccable/review/reference-ribbon-keyframes.jpg`, then made to answer the guest's finger, with champagne satin and the To‘yana gift card; the sequence is captured in `.impeccable/review/opening-sequence-0-6000ms.jpg`), with the invitation card since remade in the template's own language: the envelope's embossed paper, the gold double rule, the scene's roses and sepia script (captured in `.impeccable/review/invitation-card-pixel7.png`). The showcase gallery now names it **Atlas lenta**; the code keeps the `oltin-muhr` route and the `src/templates/muhr` directory. Everything under Colors through Components belongs to this world alone; a later template brings its own world and must not inherit these tokens, rasters, the satin bow, the seal, the invitation card or the gift card. The parts marked **Showcase shell** are the shared hosting conventions every template plugs into. The legacy app under `src/pages` and `src/components/invitation` is outside this system.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Ribboned Letter at Sunset"**

The phone screen is the closed card, edge to edge: a gatefold of the owner's ivory paper blind-embossed with roses and climbing vines, its two doors meeting at a centre seam, the inner envelope's fold lines showing faintly through toward a point at 56% height. The couple's names are printed across both doors in bronze script at 35% height, a champagne satin ribbon crosses the card at 56% and is tied at the centre in a bow, and "Lentani torting" is set in small spaced capitals near the foot. Closed, the card is alive in the guest's hands: it tilts slowly and the bow swells and rocks as if inviting a touch. A fingertip presses softly into the paper where it lands; the guest can tap, or take the ribbon and pull, and the bow and band follow the finger under rubber-band tension and spring back if let go early. Untying it is the one orchestrated moment of the page, told in order: the pulled tail is drawn out and its loop slips through, the knot gives and the other loop follows, the bow rocks free and the satin flutters down, lands and crumples away; the left door is lifted at its edge, swung open with a slight flex and settles, then the right; the inner envelope's four flaps lift and fold back off the photograph, which comes into focus as the camera moves through; the names and the welcome are written in where they will sit in the hero, and the paper dissolves into a sunset photograph of the couple walking toward a mosque, where petals keep falling and light keeps rising.

After the opening the page is ceremonial, and each section arrives once as it scrolls in: one centred phone-width column on cream (#f5ecdf), sepia ink, gold script titles, rose sprays multiplied onto the ground so their white backgrounds vanish, and an invitation card cut from the envelope's own embossed paper (quietened under a cream veil where the type sits, framed by a gold double rule, the scene's roses multiplied into two corners) that sets the Bismillah, the Rum 30:21 verse, the families, the names in sepia script, the date and the venue. The world is built from the owner's own rasters (photograph, paper, seal, roses, rings), each carrying its generation prompt and post-processing in embedded metadata; code draws what the rasters do not cover (the satin ribbon and bow, the card's fold lines, the invitation card's rules and type, gold timeline line art, dividers, the venue map, icons).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Full-bleed embossed gatefold card tied with a champagne satin bow as the first viewport; the whole card is the control (tap or pull), and it carries only the printed names and one hint line.
- An owner-supplied raster world, each image carrying embedded provenance; nothing generic or stock. The invitation card reuses the same rasters (paper and rose spray) rather than bringing its own.
- Gold is a material (foil, gilt edge, coin, pressed leaf, satin), rendered as gradients from deep antique to pale champagne, never as a flat accent fill and never on text.
- Sepia ink throughout; no black anywhere a guest can see.
- Three faces with fixed jobs: Corinthia script for names, greetings and section titles; Bodoni Moda for everything set; Amiri for the Arabic Bismillah alone.
- Slow exponential ease-out; a tied card that breathes in the hand, one choreographed untying and opening, sections that arrive once as the guest scrolls them in, two tactile interactions (pulling the ribbon, scratching the gold-foil date), otherwise only ambient motion (petals, rising light, drifting photograph, swaying roses). Reduced motion shows everything at rest.

## Colors

A warm palette of cream papers, sepia inks and a gold ramp, with amber reserved for light; even the satin ribbon is the lettering's gold softened toward the paper.

### Primary
- **Antique Gold Leaf** (gold-deep, #8f6a2a): focus outlines and caret, the scratch-card focus ring, the dark end of gilt gradients.
- **Gilt** (gold, #b08636): selected-choice border, field focus border, map arch outlines, showcase focus underline, the invitation card's double rule (1px at 0.75, the inner 0.5px line at 0.55).
- **Champagne Gold** (gold-pale, #efd9a0): the light face of gold surfaces (buttons, tiles, coin, selected choice) and the selection highlight. Button gold runs #f8e4ad, #dcb466 60%, #c59a4a at 170deg; tile gold #fbefcf, #ebcd8c 70%, #dcb46a at 165deg.
- **Script Gold** (script-gold, #9a6e2e): every Corinthia section title, the timeline times, and on the invitation card the "va" and the Bismillah.
- **Line Gold** (line-gold, #b8924a): coded line art (timeline drawings, ornamental dividers, blossom nodes, hint sparks, the invitation card's dividers and the sprigs beside "va"); matches the owner's rings drawing.
- **Bronze** (bronze, #8a6128): revealed scratch values, the hero ampersand and hero divider.

### Secondary
- **Champagne Satin** (rb1 shadow, rb2 light, rb3 mid-light, rb4 mid, rb5 highlight; `--rb1`..`--rb5` on the card): the gold of the lettering softened toward the paper, for the ribbon and bow on the closed card and nothing else. The band runs a vertical gradient (180deg: rb1, rb3 18%, rb5 40%, rb2 58%, rb4 84%, rb1) so it reads as a rounded satin band; the bow's loops, insides, knot and tails take the same five stops in their own gradients, with rb5 (and white) for the specular streaks along the loop crowns, the knot and the tail creases, and rb1 for the inside of each loop, the rims turning under and the gathered folds at the pinch. Its shadows are warm brown to match (rgba(96, 70, 30, ...)).

### Tertiary
- **Lantern Amber** (amber, #f6c76a): light only. The halo around the bow on keyboard focus (rgba(246, 199, 106, 0.85)), the rising motes over the photograph, the gold shower over the scratched dates.

### Neutral
- **Sepia Ink** (ink, #4a331b): all set text and icons on gold.
- **Faded Sepia** (ink-soft, #77604a): secondary lines, hints, labels, weekday and time, form labels; on the invitation card the verse, families, invitation line, weekday, time and address (the verse reference a paler #9a7a48). The closed card's hint is a deeper #6a5236 so it holds on the embossing.
- **Hero Ink** (hero-ink, #4f2f12): the couple's names set in the photograph's sky, darker so they hold against the sunset; the welcome lines above them use #5a3618. The invitation card's names and day numeral take the same ink.
- **Name Ink** (name-ink, #6a4818): the closing names, scroll cue, "Rahmat!" and the showcase template name.
- **Cream Ground** (ground, #f5ecdf): the column behind every section; also the colour the hero photograph fades into at its foot.
- **Envelope Paper** (paper, #f7efe2): the hero ground and the fallback under the embossed-paper print on the doors and the invitation card.
- **Paper Shade** (paper-shade, #efe4d1): the shaded end of the inner envelope's flap fronts (170deg from #fbf6ec); the side flaps run #f0e6d2 to #fbf6ec (left) and #eee3cf to #faf4e9 (right), the bottom flap #f6eee0 to #ebdfc9, the flap backs #f3e9d7 to #e6d7bd, and the inside of each door #e9ddc7, #f8f2e6 55%, #eee3cf.
- **Table Linen** (surround, #e9ddc9): the page outside the 480px column on wide screens.
- **Card Stock** (field, #fffcf5): inputs, choice buttons, ghost wash, the scratch card under its foil (#fffaf0 to #f3e6cd).
- **Gilt Hairline** (gilt-line): 1px borders on fields and choices.
- **Brick Sepia** (error-brick, #9b3b22): the single RSVP error line.
- **Gallery Linen** (showcase-ground, #efe5d4): the showcase gallery, lit from the top by a pale radial.

### Named Rules
**The Sepia Rule.** Ink is brown, never black. Even the showcase phone bezel is dark brown (#241a10). Nothing code draws is cool: the satin ribbon is champagne, and its shadows are brown; the cool notes are in the owner's photograph.

**The Light Is Not Paint Rule.** Amber and the white-gold radials describe light (the focus halo on the bow, the rising motes and the date shower, the glints that cross the foil, the invitation card and the gift card, the sky veil behind the hero names, the cream glow behind the closed card's hint). They never fill a card, a button or text.

**The Multiply Rule.** Rasters drawn on white (rose spray, rings) are laid on cream with `mix-blend-mode: multiply` so their ground disappears, on the page and on the invitation card's paper alike; the seal's initials are gold (#a77b34, dot #b88c3e) multiplied into the wax so they read as pressed, not printed.

## Typography

**Display / Script Font:** Corinthia (with Pinyon Script, cursive)
**Body Font:** Bodoni Moda with optical sizing on (with Didot, Bodoni 72, Georgia, serif)
**Arabic Font:** Amiri (with Noto Naskh Arabic, serif), loaded as a Google Fonts `text=` subset of the Bismillah only

**Character:** a hand-lettered script carries everything spoken, signed or titled; a high-contrast Didone with optical sizes carries everything set. Lining numerals throughout; tabular lining figures in the countdown.

Each size also carries a plain px fallback for browsers without container units. The parents' lines are not on the card.

### Named Rules
**The Spoken Script Rule.** Corinthia sets names, greetings, section titles and the programme times. Instructions, body copy, labels, form text and data are Bodoni Moda.

**The Bismillah Only Rule.** Amiri sets the Arabic Bismillah and nothing else, and it is fetched only for those glyphs.

**The Shares of the Card Rule.** Everything on the invitation card (type, rules, sprigs, gaps, the double rule's inset) is a share of the card's width in `cqi`, so the card scales whole like a printed one; each type size has a legible px floor (`max(px, cqi)`, nothing under 8.5px), and on the narrowest phones the card grows taller (its 3:5 ratio yields to content under `overflow: clip`) rather than letting the type shrink. Nothing inside it is sized in vw.

**The Printed Face Rule.** The closed card carries exactly two lines of text, both printed across the doors so they split at the seam: the couple's names in script and the hint line in spaced capitals. Everything else on the paper is the owner's embossing and the fold lines.

## Layout

One phone-width column, max 480px, centred on the table-linen surround with a soft ambient halo; the closed card and hero photograph fill it edge to edge at 100svh, and the page cannot scroll until the scene is revealed. On a phone the column is the screen.

The hero photograph covers the frame (`object-position: 52% 50%`). A veil lightens the sky behind the lettering (a radial at 50% 22%) and fades the floor into the cream ground from 64% to 99%. The welcome block sits in the sky from 22% to the right edge, 9% from the top plus the safe-area inset, clear of the flowering branch. Rose sprays (46% wide, rotated -12deg, one mirrored) bleed off the bottom corners.

Sections follow on cream in this order: **Sana** (scratch date, then weekday/time and countdown), **invitation card** (Bismillah when asked for, Rum 30:21, the families, both names, the invitation line, date, venue and address), **message** (with rose sprays at the sides), **To‘y dasturi** (timeline), **To‘yxona** (venue, address, arch map, map buttons), **Javobingizni kutamiz** (RSVP), **To‘yana** (the gift card, only when the couple has given a card number), **closing** (112px seal, closing line, names, long date), then the showcase end slot. Sections pad 72px by 24px, consecutive sections tighten to 24px on top; the message section pads 56px / 72px; the card section narrows sides to 12px and the card is the column less 20px, at most 420px. Inner blocks sit in 300-400px measures (scratch row and tiles 360px, gift card 340px). Grids use a 10px gap; the scratch row 12px.

Fixed controls align to the column, not the viewport: `right: max(0px, (100vw - 480px) / 2) + 16px`, and respect safe-area insets.

### Showcase shell (shared across templates)
- Each template is one hash route (`#oltin-muhr`) and plugs in as `{ Invitation, Preview }`. The Invitation accepts `replayToken` (closes and re-ties the card) and `endSlot` (rendered after its closing section).
- The gallery shows each template's closed first viewport inside a 200 x 420 phone frame (34px radius, dark brown bezel), with the template name in its own script ("Atlas lenta"), one descriptive line ("Atlas lenta bilan bog‘langan ikki eshikli karta va oqshom nuri"), and the sample couple and date. Cards lift 6px and tilt -1deg on hover with the 0.7s ease-out.
- In an example, a 44px round back arrow sits beside the column (inside it below 600px) only while the invitation is closed, and fades after 1.2s of stillness so the first filmed frame is the invitation alone.
- The end slot offers "Qaytadan ochish" (replay) as the template's own ghost button and "Barcha namunalar" as an underlined text link padded 12px 8px so its tap area reaches 44px.
- The build (`scripts/build-showcase.mjs`) emits one self-contained HTML file with JS, CSS and music inlined; fonts come from Google Fonts (Bodoni Moda with opsz axis, Corinthia, and the Amiri Bismillah subset).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is physical and photographic: real embossed paper, doors and flaps turning in perspective, a satin ribbon lying on the card, a card lying on the page, wax with a soft drop. There are no hard edges or offset shadows. Each door and inner flap turns in its own 1600px perspective with a front and a back face, and is painted in document order (top and bottom flap shadows, top and bottom flaps, side flap shadows, side flaps, door shadows, doors, ribbon), so planes never depth-sort against each other. Each inner flap lays a blurred brown shadow on what lies beneath it until it folds back; a door swinging out darkens its own front as it turns from the light and casts a shadow on the paper beside its hinge. Gold objects carry an inner top highlight, an inner gilt ring and a soft warm drop. Light (the focus halo, motes) is added with `lighter` compositing and radial gradients; text takes only soft glows (Sky halo, Hint glow), never a drop.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Column halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 80px -30px rgba(74, 51, 27, 0.35)`): the column on wide screens.
- **Gold surface** (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.65), inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(143,106,42,0.45), 0 10px 20px -12px rgba(120,84,30,0.7)`): buttons; tiles use the same recipe with a 0.35 gilt ring and a 22px drop.
- **Scratch card** (`inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(184,146,74,0.45), 0 14px 26px -16px rgba(110,76,26,0.65)`).
- **Card on paper** (`0 1px 2px rgba(80,56,24,0.18), 0 22px 40px -22px rgba(80,56,24,0.55)`): the invitation card.
- **Wax drop** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 6px 10px rgba(80,52,20,0.28))`): the closing seal.
- **Flap cast** (`rgba(90, 62, 26, 0.2)` clipped to the flap shape, `blur(4px)`, offset 3-4px toward the flap's point): each inner flap on the paper beneath it.
- **Door shadow** (`linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(60, 40, 15, 0.34), transparent)` across 55% from the hinge, mirrored for the right door): rises to full at 35% of the door's swing and fades as it completes; the door's front darkens under `rgba(60, 40, 16, 0.42)` as it turns from the light.
- **Seam** (left door `inset -1px 0 0 rgba(255, 253, 246, 0.7)` with an 8% brown fade at 0.08; right door `inset 1px 0 0 rgba(110, 80, 40, 0.3)` with a 7% fade at 0.14): the lit and shadowed edges where the doors meet.
- **Fold lines** (a 1.4px rgba(255, 253, 246, 0.95) line offset over a 0.9px rgba(120, 92, 52, 0.42) line, non-scaling): the inner envelope's folds seen through the doors, from the corners to 50% 56%.
- **Satin** (band `box-shadow: 0 2px 5px -1px rgba(96, 70, 30, 0.32)`; bow `filter: drop-shadow(0 5px 6px rgba(96, 70, 30, 0.28))`): warm brown, the ribbon only.
- **Fingertip press** (84px `radial-gradient(closest-side, rgba(110, 80, 40, 0.16), rgba(110, 80, 40, 0.06) 55%, transparent)`): a soft dent in the paper where the guest touches the closed card, growing from 0.4 to 1.25 and fading over 760ms.
- **Gift card** (`inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(176, 134, 54, 0.45), inset 0 0 0 6px rgba(255, 250, 238, 0.35), 0 18px 34px -20px rgba(96, 70, 30, 0.7)`): a gilt edge, a pale inner frame and a soft warm drop; the number carries a 1px pressed light below it (`text-shadow: 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 250, 236, 0.8)`) so it reads as stamped.
- **Bow focus** (`drop-shadow(0 0 1px #8a6128) drop-shadow(0 0 12px rgba(246, 199, 106, 0.85))`): keyboard focus on the closed card.
- **Sky halo** (`text-shadow: 0 0 22px rgba(255,243,226,0.9)`): the hero lettering, a soft light behind it, never a drop.
- **Hint glow** (`text-shadow: 0 0 3px #fbf6ec, 0 0 9px #fbf6ec, 0 0 16px rgba(251, 246, 236, 0.9)`): the closed card's hint line only, lifting it off the embossed roses beneath it.
- **Field focus** (`0 0 0 4px rgba(239, 217, 160, 0.45)` with a gilt border).
- **Stamp ring** (1.5px `rgba(184, 146, 74, 0.6)` border at 4% inset): leaves the closing seal as it is stamped (scale 0.7 to 2.8, fading, 900ms after 480ms).
- **Petal shade** (canvas `rgba(90, 55, 25, 0.18)`, blur 3, 1.5px down): the falling petals only, so they read as paper-thin objects over the photograph.

### Named Rules
**The Real Paper Rule.** The closed card's relief is the owner's embossed-paper photograph, printed across both doors as one sheet (each door shows its half of a 200%-wide print, so the image runs unbroken across the seam). The inner envelope's flap faces are cut from the same print, multiplied over their paper gradients at 0.45 so their relief is fainter. Code adds only the fold lines, the seam, the printed names and the hint line; no coded relief, flourish or colour ornament. The ribbon lies on the paper; it is not printed on it.

**The Wipe, Not Fade Rule.** A multiplied photograph (rose spray, rings) never fades and never sits inside an ancestor that fades or transforms: Chrome drops the blend mid-fade and an ancestor's opacity isolates it, so the white ground flashes. Move the image itself, not its box, and draw it in with a clip-path wipe (`inset(0 100% 0 0)` to none, 1.3s ease-out, mirrored for right-hand art). Its own rotate or scale (the sway) is fine. The invitation card is the one allowed case: its sprays multiply onto the card's own paper, inside the card's group, so the card may be laid down with opacity and transform; the sprays still wipe in (top-left from the top, bottom-right from the bottom, 1.6s after 0.9s).

## Shapes

The closed card is a gatefold: two doors, each half the width, hinged at the outer edges and meeting at the centre seam. Beneath them the inner envelope has four triangular flaps over the photograph: top and bottom reach 30% of the height to their points, the side flaps 62% of the width so they overlap, leaving diamond gaps above and below the side flaps' tips. The ribbon is a straight band 6.4% of the card width deep at 56% height; the bow is 72% of the card width, its knot 36% down the drawing and centred on the band: two loops with their darker insides showing, a pinched knot and two creased, forked tails. The invitation card is a 3:5 card (4px corners) that may grow taller on narrow phones, framed by a gold double rule 3.4cqi inside its edge with a finer second line 1.3cqi within; rose sprays 40% of its width bleed off the top-left and bottom-right corners, and the type sits centred between them (padding 11% 16% 10%). The venue map is clipped by the pointed `archPath` with a double gilt outline. Controls are pills (999px) or soft rectangles (14px), as are scratch cards and countdown tiles; the gift card is a bank card in proportion (1.586:1, 16px corners) with a 38 x 28 chip at 6px; the seal is the photographed wax disc (the closing stamp only); timeline nodes are six-petal blossoms on the ground colour; dividers are a hairline with two leaf loops around a central diamond; on the invitation card the same divider at 34cqi.

### Pull to untie (signature)
The guest can take the ribbon and pull; the satin answers under tension. The card takes no browser panning (`touch-action: none`) and captures the pointer.
- **Tension:** the bow moves toward the finger with a rubber-band falloff, giving a little and then resisting: displacement eases toward 34px across and 26px down however far the finger travels (`max * (1 - 1 / (|d| / (1.8 * max) + 1))`), and turns 0.22deg per pixel across. Each half of the band pivots on its outer end and lengthens so it stays joined to the knot; the tails turn against the pull (0.5deg per pixel, the far tail 0.6 of that) and stretch by 1 + distance / 160.
- **Release:** under 10px of travel is a tap and opens; past 56px the knot gives and it opens from the pulled side (left when the finger ended more than 4px left, otherwise right); anything between springs back to the knot over 0.75s with a slight overshoot (`cubic-bezier(0.3, 1.45, 0.5, 1)`).
- **Keyboard:** Enter or Space opens, as a tap.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep every raster owner-supplied and carrying its prompt and post-processing in embedded metadata.
- **Do** lay white-ground rasters on cream with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`, and press the seal's initials into the wax the same way.
- **Do** render gold as a gradient material with a highlight and a darker edge (deep antique to pale champagne), not as a flat fill.
- **Do** time motion with the slow ease-out (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), and tell the opening in order: the bow undone, the ribbon gone, the doors, the inner flaps, then the scene.
- **Do** let the satin answer the finger: tension that gives and then resists, a spring back with a slight overshoot when let go early, and an untying that starts from the pulled side.
- **Do** draw icons, sparks, dividers and nodes as inline SVG in line gold or currentColor.
- **Do** keep fixed controls aligned to the 480px column and clear of safe-area insets.
- **Do** size everything inside the invitation card in `cqi` of the card with a legible px floor (`max(px, cqi)`), and let the card grow taller rather than shrink its type.
- **Do** honour reduced motion: the closed card is still (no hold, bow swell or breathing hint), a tap fades it to the scene in 400ms, the hero lettering, photograph and roses stand at rest, and there are no petals, drift, sway or cue bob; every scroll reveal is shown at rest.
- **Do** bring each section in once, as the guest scrolls to it, and leave it in place.
- **Do** draw a multiplied photograph in with a clip-path wipe on the image itself.

### Don't:
- **Don't** print colour ornament or extra text on the closed card; it carries the embossed paper, its fold lines, the printed names, one hint line and the satin bow.
- **Don't** use black ink, or grey or cool tones in anything code draws; text is sepia, and even the satin ribbon is champagne.
- **Don't** fill text with a gradient or clip a gradient to text; gold text is a flat script gold or bronze.
- **Don't** use emoji or font glyphs as icons.
- **Don't** add a second automatic sequence after the opening; later motion answers the guest (a section scrolled into view, a date scratched) or is ambient.
- **Don't** fade a multiplied photograph or fade or transform any ancestor of one whose backdrop lies outside it; Chrome drops the blend and its white ground shows.
- **Don't** set instructions, body copy, labels or form text in the script face, or use Amiri for anything but the Bismillah.
- **Don't** let a later template borrow these tokens, rasters, the satin bow, the seal, the invitation card or the gift card; it shares only the showcase shell.
